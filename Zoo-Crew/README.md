# Zoo-Crew

The Zoo Crew repo secures the 19-Pillar Kessel Flow.

## Species Architecture & Roles

- **Security & Bug Honing**: Dragon hones bugs; Robot, Ghost, Cactus, and Hex-Dino harden security.
- **Boneyard Storage (2TB)**: Chonk, Owl, Jellyfish, Turtle, Snail, and Blob manage the 2TB boneyard.
- **User Interface**: Cat, Rabbit, Duck, and Goose drive the UI.
- **Pipeline Orchestration**: Octopus, Mushroom, Capybara, and Axolotl orchestrate the 18-species sync.
